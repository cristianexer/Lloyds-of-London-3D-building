The source descriptions use different floor totals (Lloyd’s says “14 floors”, RSHP lists 12). This model therefore uses a **ground level and eleven gallery labels solely as a modelling convention**, not a surveyed floor schedule. No exact height, floor area or occupancy figure is advertised in the interface.

## Asset search

The public listing [1 Lime Street, by msasurvey](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/1-lime-street-86fd85be32244490b2ba2936f16c1164) was found in search. The detail page returned HTTP 403. Its downloadable asset, licence and interior hierarchy were not verified, and no geometry was downloaded or inspected. An original procedural model avoids representing an unavailable asset as licensed or usable.

## Scope of the reconstruction

The six-tower arrangement, stepped central building, atrium, roof, silver stair/service assemblies and exposed systems are interpreted from references. Dimensions, footprint irregularities, compass orientation and connections are approximate; the model is not suitable for surveying, construction, egress or building services analysis. Positional tower names are internal labels, not official names.

Rectangular service pods, curved D-shaped stair casings, open landings, pipe collars and bends, maintenance ladders and façade supports were refined against the additional supplied close-ups. Small details are systematic interpretations rather than a claim to reproduce every real fitting.

Desk layouts, computers, radial stair treads, escalator dimensions and heritage ornament are illustrative. The Rostrum is intentionally simplified. The retained historic entrance, basements, Adam Room, neighbouring buildings, furniture on upper office levels and concealed building services are not fully reconstructed.

The site is **Lloyd’s of London, the insurance marketplace**. Labels reading “Lloyds Bank” in supplied map captures are not taken as subject identification. No banking logo or imagery is used.
